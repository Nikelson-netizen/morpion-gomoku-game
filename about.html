About Us

Hi, my name is Nikelson Sense, and I am the creator of Gomoku Online. I created this project to offer players a simple and enjoyable way to play the classic Five in a Row strategy game.

Gomoku Online includes local 2-player games, AI challenges, online multiplayer, challenge modes, and tournaments.

I continue to improve the game, add new features, and use player feedback to make the experience better over time.

My Vision

My goal with Gomoku Online is to modernize the classic Five in a Row game while keeping the simple strategy that makes Gomoku enjoyable. I wanted to transform the traditional board game into a more complete digital experience that can be played anywhere and on different devices.

Gomoku Online combines traditional gameplay with modern features such as artificial intelligence, real-time online multiplayer, player challenges, tournaments, rankings, and interactive features that bring players together.

I plan to continue developing the project by improving the online experience, adding new competitive features, and creating a stronger community around the game while preserving the original spirit of Gomoku.
