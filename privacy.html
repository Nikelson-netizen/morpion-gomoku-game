When you use Gomoku Online, certain information may be collected automatically or provided voluntarily by you.

Depending on the features you use, you may provide information such as:

  • Messages sent through the in-game chat;
  • Account information if account registration features are introduced or used.

Technical information

Our services and hosting providers may automatically process certain technical information, including:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type;
  • Device type;
  • Operating system;
  • Pages or features used;
  • Approximate connection information;

Information may be used to:

Users should not share passwords, financial information, home addresses, or other sensitive personal information through the game chat.
